Frequently asked questions

Straight answers — no sales speak.

How do I get started?

Open the Optimiser, add your variables with costs and profits, set a budget, and run the solver. The optimal quantities come back with the projected profit.

Where is my data stored?

On your own install. The app writes to local folders — your saved optimisations, exports, and uploads stay on your machine.

Can I export my results?

Yes. Invoices come out as print-ready PDFs, the finance ledger exports to CSV, and optimisation problems save and load as JSON.

Is there a free trial?

Nothing to trial — the code is open source and free to run on your own machine. No pricing tiers, no credit card, no expiry.

Still stuck?

An email is a message, and an issue is a record. Either way, it lands in the ledger.
