THESIS: Mobile Money — the everyday SA phone-banking screen built as the entire product world. The app refuses the category's SaaS-finance default (cream cards, rounded everything, corporate blue-green, decorative illustrations); it commits to a terminal-grade money interface: jet-black ground, one fresh money-green accent, hard geometric type, tabular mono for every amount, 1px ledger hairlines, and LED status dots. The operator should feel the app was built by people who sit beside a till.

OWN-WORLD: near-black graphite `#0A0F0C` ground; raised plates `#101612`; paper cream `#F4F1E8` only for light mode and the invoice/print sheet; one green `#3DDC8E` (money), LED red `#FF4F5E` (danger/overdue), amber `#FFB020`, blue `#4DA3FF`, all on tough 1px rules `rgba(255,255,255,0.12)` / warm gray on paper. Type: Space Grotesk for display and UI labels, Inter for body, Space Mono (tabular) for money and codes. Buttons are flat hard rects or green fills; borders are 1px and square; nothing is rounded beyond 6px minimum. Status is a 6px LED dot, never a badge pill.

STORY: The operator opens the app between customers and instantly reads the day: what came in, what must go out, what the Optimizer says to price next. Money is legible at a glance because every amount is a tabular figure on a ruled plate. Statuses are lights, not pills. There is zero ornament; trust comes from the register.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Finance dashboard, full-bleed black. Thin fascia: wordmark top-left (Space Grotesk 700, white, no kicker), actions top-right. Left rail: hard black nav, active item is a green-filled block with black text. Grid of four money plates (mono label, large mono value, hairline rule, status LED) then one ruled ledger card of recent transactions with right-aligned tabular R amounts and a green-filled Optimizer CTA in the rail. Nothing floats; every plate sits on hairlines.

FORM: 4 of the grounded candidate list — the SA phone-banking screen — seed key 3bdee350. Chosen as the pick card.

FINISH: reviewed and documented — this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
